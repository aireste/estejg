# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, collaborators, and employers who land on the site looking to understand who Esteban is and whether he's the right person to work with. They're typically B2B founders, marketing leaders, or startup operators who need frontend work done and want to know the person behind it — not just a resume. They scan fast, make gut decisions, and trust craft over credentials.

## Product Purpose

Esteban's personal and professional portfolio. The site exists to establish his identity as a frontend builder with real marketing analytics and B2B experience — someone who understands both the code and the funnel. Success means a visitor understands who he is, believes he can execute, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Confident, approachable, technical, friendly. The site should feel like a sharp person introducing themselves — not a polished agency, not a dry resume, not a hype machine. It earns trust through specificity and craft, not through superlatives.

## Anti-references

- AI slop: generic hero copy, stock personality, templated layouts that look like every other "developer portfolio"
- Over-animated showcases that prioritize effect over readability
- Dark tactical military aesthetics (that's SPCTR — this site is its own identity)
- Corporate LinkedIn energy
- The classic "icon + heading + three bullets" card grid

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof** — the quality of the frontend work here demonstrates frontend capability. Every detail is a portfolio piece.
2. **Specific beats generic** — real copy, real numbers, real projects. No filler, no "I'm passionate about..." boilerplate.
3. **Warm confidence** — approachable enough that a stranger feels comfortable reaching out, confident enough they don't question the capability.
4. **Restraint in motion** — animations serve the user's understanding, not the designer's ambition. One focal effect (typewriter headline) is enough.
5. **Personality over polish** — the site should feel like Esteban, not a template someone filled in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Respect prefers-reduced-motion for the typewriter and any other animations. Good contrast on the warm off-white background. No reliance on color alone to convey meaning.
